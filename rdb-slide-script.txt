TEMBERA — PRESENTATION TO RDB
Slide script for building the deck in Canva
==============================================================

DESIGN SETTINGS
  Green (main)      #1F6B4A
  Blue (planned)    #2A6B8F
  Ochre             #A8761B
  Clay              #A63A28
  Text              #101A16
  Background        #F2F4F1

  Headings: a serif — Playfair Display, Lora or Source Serif Pro. Regular weight.
  Body: a plain sans — Inter, Work Sans or Open Sans. 20pt minimum.
  Titles 40-54pt, body 20-24pt, small labels 12-14pt caps with wide spacing.

==============================================================

SLIDE 2
  Layout: title, then two columns of bullets
--------------------------------------------------------------

[SMALL LABEL]
  The gap

[TITLE]
  A visitor outside Kigali is largely on their own.

  Left column
    - Global map services cover central Kigali well, and thin out quickly beyond it.
    - Many Rwandan businesses are listed with no hours, no phone number, or nothing at all.
    - Nothing accounts for Umuganda, when much of the country closes for the morning.

  Right column
    - Visitors arriving for conferences have free evenings and no local knowledge.
    - Rwandans travelling their own country have no guide written for them.
    - There is no strong local alternative.

  SPEAKER NOTE: Keep this factual and short. RDB knows the gap better than anyone in the room — you are showing that you understand it, not teaching them.

SLIDE 6
  Layout: title, two columns of bullets (use dashes, not ticks)
--------------------------------------------------------------

[SMALL LABEL]
  Being straight with you

[TITLE]
  What today's version cannot do yet.

  Left column
    - The place data is researched, not officially verified. Real institutions, but not a checked register. This is the main reason we are here.
    - English only. Five more languages are designed and scheduled.

  SPEAKER NOTE: Do not skip or rush this. Institutions have seen many polished pitches that fell apart on contact with reality. Naming the gaps yourself is what separates you — and every one is answered by the roadmap later.


SLIDE 7
  Layout: title, three boxes across
--------------------------------------------------------------

[SMALL LABEL]
  How it is built

[TITLE]
  Built to standards a public institution can defend choosing.

  Box 1 — Data protection
  Law N° 058/2021 implemented: users can see, download and permanently delete everything held about them. A published privacy policy and a reachable contact.

  Box 2 — Security
  Passwords hashed. Sign-in attempts rate limited. Sessions expire and can be revoked. No default passwords anywhere in the system.

  Box 3 — Honesty by design
  The system never invents data. No fake ratings, no padded categories, and approximate locations are marked as approximate.


SLIDE 8
  Layout: title, subtitle, two columns of bullets. Keep this one calm and plain.
--------------------------------------------------------------

[SMALL LABEL]
  A deliberate decision

[TITLE]
  Memorial sites are never rated, reviewed, priced or promoted.

[SUBTITLE]
  Genocide memorials are in the guide, because visitors need to find them. They are not presented like a restaurant.
    - No star ratings. No reviews. No prices.
    - Never placed in "top rated" or promotional rows.
    - A restrained page design, visibly different from a listing.
    - Visiting guidance instead — dress, quiet, photography.
    - The commemoration period in April is recognised by the system.
    - Enforced in the data itself, so it cannot be undone by a design change.

  SPEAKER NOTE: Worth pausing on. The catalogue originally carried a five-star rating on a memorial site, inherited from its source data. It was found and removed deliberately. Being straightforward about that is stronger than implying it was never there. Do not decorate this slide.

SLIDE 10
  Layout: title, subtitle, two short paragraphs
--------------------------------------------------------------

[SMALL LABEL]
  PESTEL — the conclusion

[TITLE]
  Yes, it fits — but as one specific thing.

[SUBTITLE]
  Tembera works as a curated, trusted directory operating alongside the national tourism effort — serving visitors, the diaspora, and Rwandans travelling their own country.

[PARAGRAPH]
  It does not work as an advertising-funded mass-market app. Rwanda deliberately attracts fewer visitors who each spend more, so there will never be the volume that advertising requires. Recognising that shapes every decision we make.

[PARAGRAPH]
  One factor decides everything: verified place data. It is what a global search engine cannot replicate locally, what an AI assistant cannot invent, and what makes the guide worth relying on.


SLIDE 11
  Layout: title, four boxes (2×2), each with a big goal number
--------------------------------------------------------------

[SMALL LABEL]
  UN Sustainable Development Goals — direct contribution

[TITLE]
  Four goals Tembera contributes to directly.

  8 — Decent Work & Economic Growth
  Target 8.9 asks for policies promoting sustainable tourism that creates jobs and promotes local culture. Making small Rwandan businesses findable is that, directly.

  9 — Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure
  Target 9.c concerns access to information technology. This is digital tourism infrastructure, built locally rather than imported.

  11 — Sustainable Cities & Communities
  Target 11.4 asks for protection of cultural and natural heritage. Memorial and heritage sites are documented with care rather than commercialised.

  17 — Partnerships for the Goals
  Target 17.17 concerns effective public-private partnership. This proposal is exactly that: public data, private platform.

[WARNING]
  Do not use the official UN SDG logos or their exact colours — that implies endorsement you do not have. Use plain numbered boxes in your own palette.


SLIDE 12
  Layout: title, two columns — left is a list, right is two paragraphs
--------------------------------------------------------------

[SMALL LABEL]
  UN SDGs — partial, and not claimed

[TITLE]
  Where we contribute partly, and where we do not.

  Left — Partial contribution
    - Goal 10 — Reduced Inequalities. Coverage of all 30 districts pushes visibility beyond Kigali, where listings are thinnest.
    - Goal 12 — Responsible Consumption. Practical guidance for visitors, including rules they routinely get wrong.
    - Goal 5 — Gender Equality. Women-owned businesses could be identified and surfaced. Not built yet — we are not claiming it.

  Right — Not applicable
  The remaining goals — including health, water, energy, and Goal 14 on oceans, for a landlocked country — have no honest connection to a tourism directory.
  We are not claiming them. A project that maps itself onto all seventeen goals is telling you something about its marketing, not its impact.

  SPEAKER NOTE: This slide will do more for your credibility than the previous one. Everyone claims SDG alignment; almost nobody says which goals do not apply.

SLIDE 14
  Layout: title, two columns of bullets
--------------------------------------------------------------

[SMALL LABEL]
  Vision 2050 — the contribution

[TITLE]
  Rwanda markets itself extremely well abroad. The gap is what happens after arrival.
    - A visitor who finds more to do stays longer and spends more.
    - Spending spreads beyond Kigali when the guide covers all 30 districts.
    - Conference delegates are a high-value group nobody currently serves well.

SLIDE 16
  Layout: title, subtitle, three boxes, closing line. Mark PLANNED.
--------------------------------------------------------------

[SMALL LABEL]
  The vision — PLANNED

[TITLE]
  And a live picture of visitor demand, for RDB.

[SUBTITLE]
  A guide that people search generates something no survey can: a continuous record of what visitors are actually looking for, and where.

  Box 1 — Demand, by district
  Which districts are being searched, and how that moves through the year. Interest that arrives before the infrastructure does.

  Box 2 — Gaps worth filling
  Searches that return little or nothing — a direct signal of where investment or licensing attention would pay.

  Box 3 — Corrections flowing back
  Every report from a visitor or a business, shared back so the official register improves as the guide is used.

[CLOSING LINE]

  SPEAKER NOTE: Probably the most valuable slide for RDB specifically, because it offers something they cannot easily buy. Be firm on the privacy line — aggregate only, never individuals.
